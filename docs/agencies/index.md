## Keep ownership explicit

For every site, document:

- who owns the domain, hosting, WordPress.com connection, and paid licenses;
- which team receives security, backup, downtime, and billing alerts;
- who may restore, connect, disconnect, purchase, or cancel;
- the staging and production URLs;
- the support and escalation path.

The [Jetpack agency program](https://jetpack.com/for-agencies/) and [Jetpack Support](https://jetpack.com/support/) are the sources of truth for current program, product, and plan details.
