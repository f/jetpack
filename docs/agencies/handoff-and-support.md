A handoff is complete when the client knows what they own, what is monitored, and how to get help.

## Deliver the operating record

Include:

- connected account and organization ownership;
- active Jetpack products and why each is enabled;
- billing and renewal owner;
- backup and restore procedure;
- security and downtime alert recipients;
- staging and deployment workflow;
- known plugin, host, cache, or CDN interactions;
- support entitlement and contact path;
- agency escalation and response expectations.

## Verify access

Ask the client owner to sign in and confirm access. Do not mark handoff complete based only on an invitation email.

## Remove temporary access

Remove test accounts, one-time links, shared passwords, and unnecessary administrator access. Rotate secrets that were exposed during delivery according to the client's security policy.
