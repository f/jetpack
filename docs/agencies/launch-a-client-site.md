Use this checklist during final quality assurance.

## Before launch

- Confirm the production URL, environment type, and connected WordPress.com account.
- Remove staging-only settings and test accounts.
- Confirm backups complete and perform a documented restore test when the project requires it.
- Confirm security, scan, downtime, and billing alerts reach the correct people.
- Verify cache, CDN, image, and performance layers do not overlap unexpectedly.
- Exercise publishing, subscriptions, forms, social sharing, analytics, and search flows used by the site.
- Record active Jetpack products, plan ownership, renewal details, and support links.

## After launch

1. Recheck the Jetpack connection.
2. Run a production smoke test.
3. Confirm scheduled jobs, remote requests, and alerts.
4. Capture a new performance baseline.
5. Verify that the client can access the controls they own.
6. Schedule the first operational review.

Use [Hand off and support a client site](handoff-and-support.md) for the delivery package.
