Complete this planning pass before purchasing or enabling products.

## Capture requirements

Record:

- business and audience goals;
- site type and traffic profile;
- recovery time and data-loss tolerance;
- performance targets;
- publishing, newsletter, social, and analytics workflows;
- privacy, data residency, and compliance requirements;
- hosting features and current plugins;
- client budget and approval owner.

## Produce a coverage map

For each requirement, identify:

1. the system that owns it;
2. whether the host already provides it;
3. the Jetpack capability under consideration;
4. overlap or conflict risk;
5. the acceptance test;
6. the ongoing owner.

Use [Choose Jetpack products and features](../site-owners/choose-products.md) and verify current details in the [Jetpack feature reference](https://jetpack.com/support/features/).

## Define the ownership model

Decide which account will connect the site and how the client can retain control after the engagement. Avoid permanent reliance on one employee's personal account.
