Choose a growth workflow based on the relationship you want with readers.

| Need | Jetpack area | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand what people read | Stats | Useful visits, top content, referrers, and trends |
| Bring readers back | Newsletter and Subscriptions | Confirmed subscribers, opens, clicks, and unsubscribes |
| Share new content | Social | Successful posts, engagement, and editorial time saved |
| Improve discovery and speed | Performance and SEO-related features | Search visibility, Core Web Vitals, and conversion |

Use the current [Jetpack growth support path](https://jetpack.com/support/features-grow/) for feature-specific instructions.

## Create a simple review loop

1. Choose one audience goal.
2. Define one primary metric and one guardrail metric.
3. Publish consistently enough to learn.
4. Review results on a fixed schedule.
5. Change one part of the workflow at a time.

Avoid optimizing only for page views. Track the action that matters to the site, such as a subscription, purchase, donation, inquiry, or completed lesson.
