## What belongs in this path

This path explains decisions and workflows. Detailed product settings remain in the canonical [Jetpack Support documentation](https://jetpack.com/support/), where product behavior and plan details are maintained.

If your task requires PHP, JavaScript, a REST endpoint, or a WordPress hook, switch to [Build with Jetpack](../developers/index.md).
